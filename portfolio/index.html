02 About

A designer who thinks like a builder

B.Sc. in Computer Science & Engineering, Jagannath University.

My Computer Science background shaped the way I design. I think beyond individual screens, about how an experience behaves, connects, and scales.

I enjoy understanding people, exploring problems, and turning research into interfaces that feel intuitive to use and practical to build.

Because I understand how front-end code works, I design with real constraints in mind and hand off work developers can build without guesswork.

  1. 01

    Research first

    Understand the problem and the people before designing the solution.

  2. 02

    Think in systems

    Flows, states and components that hold together as the product grows.

  3. 03

    Designed to ship

    Clear specs and close collaboration with developers through handoff.

03 Off the clock

Beyond the pixels

Design is what I do, not all I am.

Away from Figma, I’m usually with my people: hunting down good food, heading out with friends, or settling in for a movie night with family. In quieter hours I draw and write. Both teach me to notice the small details, and that habit follows me into my work.
