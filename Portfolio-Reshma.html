About Me

A designer who thinks like a builder

Story behind the work

Sketching, testing, and building from ideas to interface.

My Computer Science background changed the way I approach design. I naturally think in systems—not just screens—considering how an experience behaves, connects, and scales before refining the interface. I enjoy working where user needs meet real-world constraints, turning research and ideas into products that are intuitive for people and practical for teams to build.

Research-drivenUnderstand the problem before designing the solution.
